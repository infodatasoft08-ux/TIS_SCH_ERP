Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

MITHILESH INFODATASOFT CAREER RESEARCH ORGANISATION PVT. LTD. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Times International School mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Overview of the Application

The App is a mobile ERP and management solution designed to connect students, parents, teachers, staff, and administrators to institutional services hosted at https://infodatasoft.tech. It streamlines educational management including digital attendance, homework, exams, live classes, announcements, fees management, and academic reports.

2. Information Collection and Use

While using our App, you may be asked to provide certain personally identifiable information to access secure portals. This data is handled in the following manner:

  • Login Credentials: Usernames, passwords, and optional biometric tokens used solely for secure authentication.
  • Personal & Educational Data: Student names, roll numbers, class schedules, grades, attendance logs, and fee history required for school ERP operations.
  • Data Security: All communications between the App and our backend servers are strictly encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL / HTTPS) technology.

3. Device Permissions & Capabilities

To enable complete system functionality, the App may request access to the following device features:

  • Camera Access: Used for scanning attendance QR codes and uploading profile avatar pictures.
  • Microphone & Audio Access: Leveraged during live online classes and audio interaction.
  • Push Notifications: Required to deliver instant alerts regarding homework, fee receipts, exam timetables, and emergency notices.
  • Biometric Security: Optionally used for quick local biometric (fingerprint / Face ID) authentication.
  • Network & Internet Access: Essential for accessing portal features, real-time database queries, and live content rendering.
  • Storage (Files and Media): Required to allow downloading, saving, and local caching of generated PDF reports, Excel spreadsheets, ID card templates, and exportable invoices.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, users have the right to request deletion of their personal data and account records:

  • In-App Deletion Request: You can request account data deletion directly within the App via Profile & Settings → Request Account Deletion.
  • Online Erasure Request: Users or parents may also request account erasure by submitting a formal request through our website contact form at https://infodatasoft.tech/privacy-policy or contacting school administration.
  • Data Retention: Institutional academic records (e.g. historical exam results and fee ledger audits) are retained as mandated by institutional regulatory compliance.

5. Cookies and Web Storage

The App utilizes standard secure local storage and web storage tokens to maintain session states and user preferences, preventing you from having to log in repeatedly.

6. Service Providers (Third-Party Services)

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal information with third-party marketers. We employ trusted infrastructure services (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications) strictly under confidentiality agreements.

7. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. All network traffic is encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols. While no electronic transmission is 100% secure, we implement industry-standard commercial safeguards to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services process student data for educational purposes under the supervision of educational institutions, parents, or legal guardians. If you become aware that unauthorized personal data has been submitted, please contact us for prompt removal.
