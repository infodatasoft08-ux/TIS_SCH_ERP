Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

Mithilesh Infodatasoft ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Times International School mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Overview of the Application

The App is a secure web-wrapper designed to connect users to our enterprise cloud solutions, accessible via https://infodatasoft.tech. It streamlines access to portal services including educational data management, exam checks, registration records, administrative reports, file downloads, sharing, and print management.

2. Information Collection and Use

While using our App, you may be asked to provide certain personally identifiable information to access secure portals. This data is handled in the following manner:

  • Login Credentials: Usernames and passwords are used solely for authentication and secure session maintenance with the servers.
  • Personal & Educational Data: Any registration details, grades, roll numbers, or employee roles entered are used directly for portal features.
  • Data Security: All communications between the App and the server are encrypted using secure Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS).

3. Device Permissions & Capabilities

To enable complete system functionality, the App may request access to the following device features:

  • Network & Internet Access: Essential for accessing portal features, real-time database queries, and content rendering.
  • Storage (Files and Media): Required to allow downloading, saving, and local caching of generated PDF reports, Excel spreadsheets, ID card templates, and exportable invoices.
  • File Sharing & Printing APIs: Leveraged to let you print generated documents directly to local or network printers, or share them securely with external applications.

4. Cookies and Web Storage

The App utilizes standard cookies and local HTML5 web storage to maintain session states and user preferences, preventing you from having to log in repeatedly. You can manage web cache or storage settings via your device operating system.

5. Service Providers (Third-Party Services)

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal information with third-party marketers. We may employ third-party services only to facilitate portal hosting, database maintenance, or file-sharing libraries, which operate under strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13 without verifiable parental or institutional consent. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us so we can perform necessary removal actions.
